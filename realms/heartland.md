# Heartland

The realm the world plays in today (#865): an assortment of instances, each built from its own region pieces. The picture shows where they lie relative to each other, and neighbouring instances are the ones an exit joins: the glade in the middle, the forest to its northwest with the castle west of it, the outpost to the glade's southeast and the mountain pass southwest of the outpost.

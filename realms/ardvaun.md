# Ardvaun

The realm the world plays in today (#865), named in Land-speech on #909 (#974): an assortment of instances, each built from its own region pieces. The picture shows where they lie relative to each other, and an exit leads to the instance drawn beside its own on the exit's side: Lumwen Hoal in the middle, Morwe Wudhen to its northwest with Dhorvone Stolm west of it, Edhra Tewk to Lumwen Hoal's southeast and Brekhal Varn southwest of Edhra Tewk. The players start in Lumwen Hoal (#712).

The moss, pondweed, feather and bone brews take effect: a Regrowth heals its share of full HP every second, a Ward adds its guard to every hit's, a Quicken shortens a companion's wait between strikes and a Fortify scales its strikes, each for its time and one at a time per target; a Mend for a target at full HP is refused ([#2311](https://github.com/Coarisfinia/khovax/issues/2311)).

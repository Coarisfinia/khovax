A wild monster's day follows from its species' traits and its entity id instead of rolls: it goes to sleep its id modulo the bedtime spread hours after dusk or dawn (or into each quarter, for a cathemeral one), and its needs take evenly spaced hours of its waking day from the hour it wakes, taking turns ([#2238](https://github.com/Coarisfinia/khovax/issues/2238)).

# Brekhal Varn: high shelf

Brekhal Varn's clearing (#2327): a broad shelf of open ground high above the pass, where no wild monster roams, far enough from all of them that Brekhal Varn's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872). A disc of radius 7 with a few boulders and shrubs, and rock walls at its northwest and southeast, it ends a way, joining the rest of the pass to the east only, in the middle of an edge, so its neighbour meets it along the whole edge (#689) and nothing lies beyond it. The opened pass (brekhal_varn_opened) shares it.

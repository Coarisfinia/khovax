# Brekhal Varn: sealed passage

A side passage off the pass (#689), choked with boulders: Ausvren vo Tormaesk's seal (#1590), which only they open, once a player has led them to it (#1588). Behind it the way leads southwest into Hrovumel Stolm, the dungeon below the pass (#1591). The guide waits on the pass's far side, the passage's mouth is marked as a place, and a wild monster stands in the pass. Joins the pass on its east and west edges.

# Brekhal Varn: switchback

A switchback in the pass (#689): the path doubles back between rock walls, a wild monster on the bend and a Kalvrakkor above it (#1692), a treasure chest tucked against the boulders below the bend and loot on the ground beside them, both of which may hold the rime shard a Kavrikka needs to ascend (#1733) or the wind ribbon a Tikazzelu needs (#1732). Joins the pass on its east and west edges.

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `⬛`: rock
- `👾`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the west
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🪨`: boulder
- `🐕`: a wild monster, always kalvrakkor
- `🧰`: a treasure chest on the ground
- `💰`: loot on the ground

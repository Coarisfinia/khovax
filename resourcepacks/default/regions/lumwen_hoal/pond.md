# Lumwen Hoal: pond

A pond in Lumwen Hoal (#57): water in the middle, ground around it, a treasure chest on its far bank (#124), a little forest (#116) on its south side with stumps and shrubs (#205), a wild monster hiding in the shrubs by the boulder (#202), and a boulder by the chest. A disc of radius 5 (#712), it joins Lumwen Hoal in the middle of its east and west edges, so its neighbours meet it along the whole edge (#689).

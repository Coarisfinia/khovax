# Lumwen Hoal: clearing

Lumwen Hoal's clearing (#2323): an open meadow where no wild monster grazes, far enough from all of them that Lumwen Hoal's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872). A disc of radius 7 with a few shrubs, a boulder and a stump, and trees at its rim, it ends a way, joining the rest of Lumwen Hoal to the west only, in the middle of an edge, so its neighbour meets it along the whole edge (#689) and nothing lies beyond it.

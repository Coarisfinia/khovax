# Lumwen Hoal: meadow

An open meadow in Lumwen Hoal (#712): two wild monsters graze in the open, each with room to walk a ring around it, among shrubs (#205), a boulder and a stump, with a few trees (#116) on its west side. A disc of radius 5, it joins Lumwen Hoal in the middle of its east and west edges, so its neighbours meet it along the whole edge (#689).

# Dhorvone Stolm: outer bailey

Dhorvone Stolm's outer bailey (#2328): the wide yard inside the castle's fallen outer wall, where no wild monster keeps watch, far enough from all of them that Dhorvone Stolm's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872). A disc of radius 7 with stubs of the old wall at its rim, a few boulders, stumps and shrubs, and a crate left behind, it ends the way, joining the rest of Dhorvone Stolm to the southeast only, in the middle of an edge, so its neighbour meets it along the whole edge (#689) and nothing lies beyond it.

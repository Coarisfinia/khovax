# Morwe Wudhen: clearing

Morwe Wudhen's clearing (#2325): a wide opening in the deep wood where no wild monster roams, far enough from all of them that Morwe Wudhen's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872). A disc of radius 7, old stumps where trees were felled long ago, a few shrubs and a boulder, ringed by the trees of the wood. It ends a way, joining the rest of Morwe Wudhen on one edge only, in the middle of that edge, so its neighbour meets it along the whole edge (#689) and nothing lies beyond it.

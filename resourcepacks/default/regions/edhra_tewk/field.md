# Edhra Tewk: back field

Edhra Tewk's back field (#2326): open ground behind the camp, kept free of wild monsters, with a patch of sand in its middle, a broken ring of stumps around it and a few crates, room for a standing stone well away from every wild monster (#1872). A disc of radius 7 that ends the way, it joins Edhra Tewk on its northwest edge only.

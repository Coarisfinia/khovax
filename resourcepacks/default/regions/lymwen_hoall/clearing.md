# Lymwen Hoall: clearing

Lymwen Hoall's clearing (#2332): the far end of the felling, where the corruption's monster never strays, far enough from it that Lymwen Hoall's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872), as Lumwen Hoal's does in its own clearing (#2323). A disc of radius 7 of bare ground with a few felled stumps and rocks, and rock at its rim, it ends the way, joining the start piece to the west only, in the middle of an edge, so the two meet along the whole edge (#689) and nothing lies beyond it.

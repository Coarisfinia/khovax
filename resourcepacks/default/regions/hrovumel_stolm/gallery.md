# Hrovumel Stolm: gallery

The gallery of Hrovumel Stolm (#1591): a long hall of rock pillars, old crates rotting along its walls, and two wild monsters. Joins Hrovumel Stolm on its east and west edges.

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `⬛`: rock
- `👾`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the west
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🪨`: boulder
- `📦`: crates

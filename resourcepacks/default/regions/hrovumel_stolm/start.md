# Hrovumel Stolm: way in

Hrovumel Stolm's start piece (#1591): the way in from the guide's seal, a hall cut into the rock. Its top edge is the way back out across three tiles, northeast to Brekhal Varn and the opened seal (#1590); players 1 to 4 begin beside it. A wild monster waits further in, and it joins the rest of Hrovumel Stolm to the west across a whole edge.

A passage leads southeast from it into the great hall (#2330): a wide vault on six pillars around a still pool, old crates and fallen stones by its walls, where no wild monster prowls. It lies off the way to the shrine, away from the rest of the stronghold, so Hrovumel Stolm's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872). It's part of the way in, not a piece of its own, because the dungeon's pieces form one way that ends at the shrine (RegionArranger): an end piece of its own would find no open edge left.

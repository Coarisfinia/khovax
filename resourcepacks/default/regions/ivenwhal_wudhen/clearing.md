# Ivenwhal Wudhen: clearing

Ivenwhal Wudhen's clearing (#2331): a wide floor of mana moss where no wild monster roams, far enough from all of them that Ivenwhal Wudhen's standing stone stands there, at least 15 tiles from every wild monster's spot (#1872). A path through the trees leads southeast from the gap in the start piece's gems, three tiles wide where it meets it (#689), into a disc of radius 6 walled by tall gems on the side towards the wood and by trees on its far side, with an outcrop of gems in its middle, a few trees and three thick patches of moss where players gather moss by hand (#1490). It ends a way, joining the start piece to the west only, and nothing lies beyond it. The path bends southeast rather than running straight east, so the clearing keeps clear of Edhra Tewk to the northeast and of Hrovumel Stolm to the southwest in the world's layout (#187).

# Basic

The playground and the first instance: a disc of radius 3, ground with a beach of sand at its southwest by the water (#321), the place the first quest leads to. Digits mark where players 1 to 4 start, and m where a wild monster stands (a ball for now, #19), with room to walk a ring around it. Its east edge leads to the glade (#140).

## Tiles

```
     . . . .
    . . . . x
   . . . 1 . .
  . m 3 . 4 . .
 ~ . . 2 . . .
~ ~ s s s . .
 ~ ~ s s s s
```

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `~`: water
- `s`: sand; stepping here reaches the place beach
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `m`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `x`: exit to the east, to glade

# Basic

The playground: a disc of radius 3, all ground. Digits mark where players 1 to 4 start, and m where a wild monster stands (a ball for now, #19), with room to walk a ring around it. Its east edge leads to the glade (#140).

## Tiles

```
   . . . .
  . . . . x
 . . . 1 . .
. m 3 . 4 . .
 . . 2 . . .
  . . . . .
   . . . .
```

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `m`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `x`: exit to the east, to glade

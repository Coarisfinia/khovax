# Castle: gatehouse

The ruined castle's start piece (#689): the corrupted castle of #574, not corrupted yet. A broken ring of wall around the gatehouse yard, rubble inside it and a wild monster on guard. Its east edge leads back to the forest across three tiles, and it joins the rest of the castle to the northwest across a whole edge. Players 1 to 4 begin by the way in.

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the northwest
- `x`: exit to the east, to forest
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `🪵`: stump
- `🪨`: boulder

# Castle: towers

Two fallen towers (#689), each a ring of wall with a gap, with shrubs grown over the ruins and a storm finch between them. Joins the castle on its northwest and southeast edges.

## Tiles

```
     . . . . . .
    . . ^ . . . .

 . ^ . . . 🪵 . ^ . .
  . . . . . . . . k
   . . 🪵 . . . . .

     . 🌳 . 🪵 . .
```

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the northwest
- `k`: joins another piece to the southeast
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `🪵`: stump
- `g`: a wild monster hiding in shrubs
- `F`: a wild monster, always storm_finch

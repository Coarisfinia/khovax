# Arena

The arena (#127): one region piece that is never turned, the way a boss arena keeps its layout. A disc of radius 3 with rocks at its corners, the players on one side and a wild monster across, always a storm finch: the arena names its boss's species (#133).

## Tiles

```
   ^ . . ^
  . . B . .
 . . . . . .
. . . . . . .
 . . 1 2 . .
  . 3 4 . .
   ^ . . ^
```

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `^`: rock
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `B`: a wild monster, always storm_finch

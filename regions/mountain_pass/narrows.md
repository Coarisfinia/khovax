# Mountain pass: narrows

The narrows (#689): the pass at its tightest, three tiles wide, widening where a storm finch nests. Joins the pass on its east and west edges.

## Tiles

```
     ^ ^ ^ ^ . .
    ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ . .
   ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ . . k
  ^ ^ ^ . . . . . .
 . ^ ^ . F . . . . .
. . ^ . . . . . ^ . .
 . . . . . . ^ ^ ^ .
  . . . . . ^ ^ ^ ^
   j . . ^ ^ ^ ^ ^
    . . ^ ^ ^ ^ ^
     . . ^ ^ ^ ^
```

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the west
- `k`: joins another piece to the east
- `F`: a wild monster, always storm_finch

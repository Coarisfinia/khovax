# Mountain pass: foot

The mountain pass's start piece (#689): the foot of the pass, a valley between rock walls. Its northeast edge leads back to the outpost across three tiles, and it joins the rest of the pass to the west across a whole edge. Players 1 to 4 begin at the way in.

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the west
- `x`: exit to the northeast, to outpost
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `🪨`: boulder

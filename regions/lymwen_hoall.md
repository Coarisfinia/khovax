# Lymwen Hoall

Lymwen Hoall (#541): the variant the flag lumwen_hoal_corrupted chooses for Lumwen Hoal, a placeholder until corrupted instances get their own look. A bare clearing of felled stumps and rock, a disc of radius 5 like Lumwen Hoal's start piece (#712), with the ways out where that piece has them (#689): north to Morwe Wudhen and southeast to Edhra Tewk, each three tiles wide, so the web of instances lies the same around it. In its middle the wild monster holding the corruption, with room to walk a ring around it, and southwest of it where players 1 to 4 start, since Lumwen Hoal is where the world starts and a player out of HP is sent back to (#727). When it faints it emits lumwen_hoal_purified, whose world flag turns Lumwen Hoal back into itself the next time nobody is in it.

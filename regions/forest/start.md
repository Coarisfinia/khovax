# Forest: edge

The forest's start piece (#689): where the trees begin, north of the glade. Its south edge leads back to the glade across three tiles, its west edge on to the castle across three more, and it joins the rest of the forest to the north across a whole edge. Players 1 to 4 begin by the way in. Trees and boulders stand where harvesting (#675) can take them later, a wild monster waits in the open and another hides in the trees.

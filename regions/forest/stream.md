# Forest: stream

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the northeast
- `k`: joins another piece to the southwest
- `🌲`: forest
- `🪨`: boulder
- `g`: a wild monster hiding in shrubs
- `T`: a wild monster, always moss_toad

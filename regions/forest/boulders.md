## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `👾`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the northeast
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the southwest
- `🌲`: forest
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `🪨`: boulder
- `🐦`: a wild monster, always tikazzir

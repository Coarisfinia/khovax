# Forest: felling

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the northeast
- `k`: joins another piece to the southwest
- `🌲`: forest
- `🪵`: stump
- `📦`: crates
- `h`: a wild monster hiding in forest
- `W`: a wild monster, always moss_toad, wounded

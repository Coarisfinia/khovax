# Forest: felling

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the northeast
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the southwest
- `🌲`: forest
- `🪵`: stump
- `📦`: crates
- `🦉`: a wild monster hiding in forest
- `🤕`: a wild monster, always moss_toad, wounded

# Glade: start

The glade's start piece (#57), where the world starts (#712): a disc of radius 5 where players 1 to 4 begin, with the way north to the forest and the way southeast to the outpost, each three tiles wide (#689). It joins the rest of the glade in the middle of its east edge, so the next piece meets it along the whole edge. A beach of sand runs down to the water at its southwest, the place the first quest leads to, where a wounded Ombulumo waits on the sand among broken crates to be dominated (#321, #446, #452). A lookout north of the middle reveals the glade around it (#181), a stump, turned by hand (#231) so it doesn't look like the pond's, stands at its northwest, and a scout of the Amvrel Velmarai waits east of the start for the players to talk to (#557, #618).

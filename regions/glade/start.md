# Glade: start

The glade's start piece (#57), where the world starts (#712): a disc of radius 5 where players 1 to 4 begin, with the way north to the forest and the way southeast to the outpost, each three tiles wide (#689). It joins the rest of the glade in the middle of its east edge, so the next piece meets it along the whole edge. A beach of sand runs down to the water at its southwest, the place the first quest leads to, where a wounded moss toad waits on the sand among broken crates to be dominated (#321, #446, #452). A lookout north of the middle reveals the glade around it (#181), and a stump, turned by hand (#231) so it doesn't look like the pond's, stands at its northwest.

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `~`: water
- `🌲`: forest
- `🪓`: stump, turned 3
- `s`: sand; stepping here reaches the place beach
- `📦`: crates; stepping here reaches the place beach
- `w`: a wild monster, always moss_toad, wounded, on sand
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `j`: joins another piece to the east
- `f`: exit to the northwest, to forest
- `o`: exit to the southeast, to outpost
- `L`: a lookout; stepping here reveals within 8

# Glade: start

The glade's start piece (#57): a disc of radius 2 where players 1 to 4 begin, with the way back west to the basic instance (#140), the way north to the forest and the way southeast to the outpost, each three tiles wide (#689). It joins the rest of the glade in the middle of its east edge, so the next piece meets it along the whole edge. A lookout north of the middle reveals the glade around it (#181), and a stump, turned by hand (#231) so it doesn't look like the pond's, stands at its northwest.

## Tiles

```
  f f f
 🪓 🌲 L j
x . 1 3 o
 x 2 . o
  x 4 o
```

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `🌲`: forest
- `🪓`: stump, turned 3
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `j`: joins another piece to the east
- `x`: exit to the west, to basic
- `f`: exit to the northwest, to forest
- `o`: exit to the southeast, to outpost
- `L`: a lookout; stepping here reveals within 8

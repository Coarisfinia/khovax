# Glade: start

The glade's start piece (#57): a disc of radius 2 where players 1 to 4 begin, open on two sides, with the way back west to the basic instance (#140).

## Tiles

```
  k . .
 . . . .
x . 1 . j
 . 2 . 3
  . 4 .
```

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `1` to `4`: where players 1 to 4 start
- `j`: joins another piece to the east
- `x`: exit to the west, to basic
- `k`: joins another piece to the northwest

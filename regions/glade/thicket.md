# Glade: thicket

A thicket in the glade (#57): a wild monster in the middle with room to walk a ring around it, rock at two corners, a boulder and shrubs (#205) on its west side and forest (#116) along its east side, open on three sides.

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `🌲`: forest
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `🪨`: boulder
- `^`: rock
- `m`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `j`: joins another piece to the northwest
- `k`: joins another piece to the east
- `l`: joins another piece to the southwest

# Glade: hollow

A rocky hollow in the glade (#712): rock rims its corners and ridges cross its middle, boulders lie about, and a wild monster stands at the edge of the trees on its west side, with room to walk a ring around it. A disc of radius 5, it joins the glade in the middle of its east and west edges, so its neighbours meet it along the whole edge (#689).

# Glade: hollow

A rocky hollow in the glade (#712): rock rims its corners and ridges cross its middle, boulders lie about, and a wild monster hides in the trees (#202) on its west side. A disc of radius 5, it joins the glade in the middle of its east and west edges, so its neighbours meet it along the whole edge (#689).

## Legend

- `.`: ground
- `🌲`: forest
- `🪨`: boulder
- `^`: rock
- `h`: a wild monster hiding in forest
- `j`: joins another piece to the east
- `k`: joins another piece to the west

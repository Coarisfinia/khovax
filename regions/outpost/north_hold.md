# Outpost: north hold

One of the outpost's holds (#689): open sand in a ring of stumps with its middle marked as a place, where a control point (#643) can stand later, and wild monsters around it. Joins the outpost on its northwest and southeast edges.

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the southeast
- `k`: joins another piece to the northwest
- `P`: a hold to take later; stepping here reaches the place outpost_north_hold
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `🪵`: stump
- `:`: sand
- `T`: a wild monster, always moss_toad

# Outpost: yard

The outpost's yard (#689): a sandy square with crates in the middle, posts of stumps around it and two wild monsters. Room for faction buildings later (#640). Joins the outpost on its northwest and southeast edges.

## Legend

- `j`: joins another piece to the southeast
- `k`: joins another piece to the northwest
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `📦`: crates
- `:`: sand
- `F`: a wild monster, always storm_finch

# Outpost: gate

The faction outpost's start piece (#689): its gate, a palisade of stumps to the northeast, stacked crates and a sandy drill yard. Its northwest edge leads back to the glade across three tiles, its southwest edge on to the mountain pass across three more, and it joins the rest of the outpost to the southeast across a whole edge. Players 1 to 4 begin by the gate. Factions (#637) take it over later.

# Outpost: watch

The outpost's watch (#689): a lookout in a ring of rock with one way in, which reveals the outpost around it, the last of its places to hold (#643, #645). A wild monster prowls by it and another hides in the shrubs. Joins the outpost on its northwest and southeast edges.

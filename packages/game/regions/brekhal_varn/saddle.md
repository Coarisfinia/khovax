# Brekhal Varn: saddle

The saddle at the top of the pass (#689): a wide hollow between the peaks, with boulders, a few shrubs and two wild monsters, one of them a Vynakiir, the mana wolf (#1167). Joins the pass on its east and west edges.

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `⬛`: rock
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the west
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `🪨`: boulder
- `🐸`: a wild monster, always ombulumo
- `🐺`: a wild monster, always vynakiir

# Brekhal Varn: narrows

The narrows (#689): the pass at its tightest, three tiles wide, widening where a Tikazzir nests. Joins the pass on its east and west edges.

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `⬛`: rock
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the west
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🐦`: a wild monster, always tikazzir

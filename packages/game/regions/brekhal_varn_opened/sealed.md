# Brekhal Varn: sealed passage, opened

The sealed passage once Ausvren vo Tormaesk has opened their seal (#1590): Brekhal Varn's variant brekhal_varn_opened, which the world flag their opening sets chooses, so the way into Hrovumel Stolm (#1591) stays open after the instance is purged. Its boulders are cleared to the ground beneath, and the guide stands beside the passage's mouth. The rest of the piece is the sealed passage's (regions/brekhal_varn/sealed.md), and every other piece of the pass is Brekhal Varn's own.

- `🟫`: ground

- `👾`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list

- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🧙`: a person to talk to, ausvren_vo_tormaesk
- `🚧`: the mouth of a sealed passage; stepping here reaches the place sealed_passage
- `🌀`: exit to the southwest

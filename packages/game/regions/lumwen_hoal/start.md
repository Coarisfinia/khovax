# Lumwen Hoal: start

Lumwen Hoal's start piece (#57), where the world starts (#712): a disc of radius 5 where players 1 to 4 begin, with the way north to Morwe Wudhen and the way southeast to Edhra Tewk, each three tiles wide (#689). It joins the rest of Lumwen Hoal in the middle of its east edge, so the next piece meets it along the whole edge. A beach of sand runs down to the water at its southwest, the place the first quest leads to, where a wounded Ombulumo waits on the sand among broken crates to be dominated (#321, #446, #452), far enough up from the water that a ring of one closes round it where it sleeps (#1379). A lookout north of the middle reveals Lumwen Hoal around it (#181), a stump, turned by hand (#231) so it doesn't look like the pond's, stands at its northwest, and Teska na Amvrel, a scout of the Amvrel Velmarai, waits east of the start for the players to talk to (#557, #618).

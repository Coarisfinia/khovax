# Hrovumel Stolm: way in

Hrovumel Stolm's start piece (#1591): the way in from the guide's seal, a hall cut into the rock. Its top edge is open across three tiles, where #1590 puts the exit back to the seal's instance; players 1 to 4 begin beside it. A wild monster waits further in, and it joins the rest of Hrovumel Stolm to the west across a whole edge.

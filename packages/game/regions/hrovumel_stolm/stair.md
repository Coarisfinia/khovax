# Hrovumel Stolm: stair

The stair of Hrovumel Stolm (#1591): a passage winding down through the rock between fallen boulders, a Tikazzir on the landing. Joins Hrovumel Stolm on its east and west edges.

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `⬛`: rock
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the west
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🪨`: boulder
- `🐦`: a wild monster, always tikazzir

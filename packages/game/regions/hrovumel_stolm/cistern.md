# Hrovumel Stolm: cistern

The cistern of Hrovumel Stolm (#1591): a flooded hall, its floor broken by still pools, where a Vynakiir drinks and another wild monster prowls. Joins Hrovumel Stolm on its east and west edges.

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `⬛`: rock
- `🟦`: water
- `👾`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the west
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the east
- `🐺`: a wild monster, always vynakiir

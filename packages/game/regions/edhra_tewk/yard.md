# Edhra Tewk: yard

## Legend

- `🟫`: ground
- `👾`: a wild monster, of a species rolled from the instance blueprint's list
- `🔗`: joins another piece to the southeast
- `🧷`: joins another piece to the northwest
- `🌳`: shrubs
- `📦`: crates
- `🟨`: sand
- `🐦`: a wild monster, always tikazzir

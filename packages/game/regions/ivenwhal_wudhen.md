# Ivenwhal Wudhen

Ivenwhal Wudhen (#1218), the mana forest: the home of Vynakiir, the arcane wolf (#1219), a wood of violet mana moss ringed by large gems, one region piece played as it is drawn. Its top edge leads back to Brekhal Varn across three tiles, where players 1 to 4 begin. A pack of Vynakiir roams its moss, and another of them hides in the trees. A pond lies in its southwest, and an open glade of moss in its middle, where the wolves' schedule finds water and grass later (#1201, #1203).

# Input Prompts 1.5A

- **Author:** Kenney (www.kenney.nl)
- **Source:** https://kenney.nl/assets/input-prompts (`kenney_input-prompts_1.5.zip`:
  `Keyboard & Mouse/Vector/keyboard_enter.svg`, `keyboard_escape.svg`, `keyboard_tab.svg`,
  `keyboard_1.svg` to `keyboard_3.svg`, `keyboard_equals.svg`, `keyboard_minus.svg`,
  `keyboard_e.svg`, `keyboard_q.svg`, `keyboard_r.svg`, `keyboard_t.svg`, `keyboard_v.svg`,
  `keyboard_x.svg` and `mouse_left.svg`, `Touch/Vector/touch_tap.svg`, `touch_zoom_in.svg` and
  `touch_zoom_out.svg`, and from the `Vector/` folder of the pack's controller set whose face
  buttons read A, B, X and Y its A, B, X and Y buttons, its view and menu buttons, its shoulder
  buttons, its triggers and its d-pad pressed each way, renamed `controller_button_a.svg` to
  `controller_button_y.svg`, `controller_button_back.svg`, `controller_button_start.svg`,
  `controller_button_left_shoulder.svg`, `controller_button_right_shoulder.svg`,
  `controller_trigger_left.svg`, `controller_trigger_right.svg` and `controller_dpad_left.svg`,
  `_up`, `_right` and `_down`, after Godot's names for them)
- **License:** CC0 1.0, see `LICENSE.md` next to this file (the pack's own notice). Credit is
  welcome, not required.
- **Used for:** the glyphs that show which key or button does what, for the device in use (#583):
  the dialog's buttons and the menu's close button, the action buttons (#1185), the roster's
  squares, the Survey's buttons on the screen and in the menu, and the menu's open button (#1192);
  the click and the tap that swap on the roster's squares, and the pinch that zooms the Survey
  (#1221); the focus keys beside the focus ring (#1210).
- **Only what the game uses is here:** those glyphs, unchanged but for the controller ones'
  names and the d-pad's and the mouse's colors: the pack marks the pressed arm or button in red,
  which the ink the glyphs are drawn in hides, so it is solid white and the rest faint (#1192,
  #1221), a difference of shape rather than color.

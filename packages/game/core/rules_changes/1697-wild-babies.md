Wild bundles hold babies: each bundle of rolled wild monsters (gathered the way a pack gathers) has a 60% chance that its last half, at least one, start as babies of the first form of their line, level 1 and unevolved, in the first form's own look; a Vynakiir's baby is a Vynnimu ([#1697](https://github.com/Coarisfinia/khovax/issues/1697)).

Pack monsters band together: wild monsters of a `pack_species` species band with their own species, `pack_type` ones also with a similar typing, up to four to a pack; members live by their leader's day and keep near it in the hours they follow their pack, and a pack whose leader is dominated or faints follows its next member ([#1205](https://github.com/Coarisfinia/khovax/issues/1205)).
